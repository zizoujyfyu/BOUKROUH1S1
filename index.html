Palestine: A Struggle for Freedom and Justice

Palestine is not just a land; it is a symbol of struggle, resistance, and the unbreakable spirit of its people. For over 75 years, Palestinians have endured occupation, displacement, and violence. Their homes have been destroyed, their voices silenced, and their futures threatened—but they remain resilient.

The Palestinian people are living under conditions that challenge the very basics of human rights. From restricted movement and military blockades to limited access to clean water and education, they fight daily for their dignity and survival. Yet, amid this suffering, they continue to raise their flag high, holding on to hope and faith that justice will one day prevail.

Palestinians are not just victims—they are artists, doctors, teachers, farmers, and children who dream of peace. Their struggle represents a global call for freedom, equality, and human rights.

Countries Supporting Palestine

  • Turkey - Actively supports Palestine diplomatically and sends humanitarian aid.
  • Qatar - Provides financial and medical assistance to Gaza.
  • Jordan - Strong historical and political ties with the Palestinian people.
  • Malaysia - Vocal supporter in international platforms.
  • South Africa - Draws parallels with its own apartheid struggle.

Countries Against Palestine

  • Israel - Main force of occupation and military aggression.
  • United States - Political and military support for Israel.
  • United Kingdom - Mixed stance, often supports Israeli policies.

Suffering of the People

The children of Gaza wake to the sound of airstrikes. Families are torn apart, with loved ones imprisoned or killed. Medical supplies are scarce, power outages are common, and economic hardship is widespread.

Entire generations have grown up in refugee camps, longing for their ancestral homes. Despite all odds, the Palestinian people continue to resist through education, culture, and their unwavering love for their homeland.
